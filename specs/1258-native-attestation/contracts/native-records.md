# Native producer contract

The supported producer writes JSONL under the account-derived default `.codex/sessions` root. The first session_meta belongs to the child. Full-history forks may retain parent session_meta records at or before the fork boundary; those records cannot replace child identity or prove reports or settings. A second session_meta after that boundary is rejected. The parent collaboration.spawn_agent function_call, JSON-string function_call_output.task_name, child own assistant final_answer, optional later task_complete and own turn_context form the evidence contract.

Hook transport may name the tool spawn_agent or collaborationspawn_agent. The genuine SubagentStop carries session_id, agent_id, agent_type, transcript_path, agent_transcript_path and last_assistant_message. Its UUID maps to canonical path only after matching protected records. An outside root, malformed record, mismatch, inherited final or earlier replay cannot attest.

Native parent agent_message notifications suppress repeated delivery only with the matching author, recipient, exact FINAL_ANSWER frame, body and timestamp. Companion jobs and ordinary owner replies do not satisfy this contract.

Persisted seat identity keeps ordinary canonical paths unchanged. If a recognized credential or configured canary occurs in an identity, both launch and verified native stop use `sha256-` plus the SHA256 of the complete raw identity. This avoids masking distinct paths to one key. Native report filenames hash that persisted key. Notification proof still requires the exact raw canonical sender and frame; matching applies the same identity mapping before comparing the filename key. Proof correlates raw protected identities before privacy mapping.
