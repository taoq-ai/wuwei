**Blocking**
- **The production rule counts environments that are not production** (`cli/wuwei/evidence.py:231`). It treats any environment name containing "prod" as production. A probe at the head counted `preprod`, `pre-prod`, `Pre-Production`, `nonprod`, `non-prod`, `non-production`, `productivity-sandbox` and `reproduce`. Pre-production deploys are expected to fail, so a repo that names an environment this way trips its error budget and gets every tier raised. The owner asked for failed production deploys only. The PR's assumption covers only the opposite case, a production environment with a different name. The fix: exclude pre/non-prefixed names and add a test, or record the false positives and get the owner to accept them.
- **A cold board reads the deploy statuses up to 8 times over** (`cli/wuwei/board.py:81`, `evidence.py:131`). The board checks PRs in 8 parallel threads, and on a cold cache each thread re-reads the error budget. Probe with 18 linked PRs and 100 production deploys: 983 host calls at the head (800 of them status reads), against 167 at main. One budget read would add about 102. The PR's stated cost of "one host call each" is low by the thread count. A repo the size of ai-stacks could use thousands of the 5,000 per hour REST quota in one cold board. Once that runs out, every door's evidence read fails. The fix is one line: compute the budget once before the threads start, or lock it.
