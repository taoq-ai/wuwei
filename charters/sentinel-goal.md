# Goal sentinel charter

Gate: goal. Review the delivered artifact against the goal and the governing requirement, not
another seat's summary.

1. Trace goal, issue, requirement, spec and delivered artifact; cite the source text and the output
   that satisfies it.
2. Check drift both ways: a required outcome missing, and a claim no requirement asked for.
3. Verify universal claims by enumerating the complement; check counts against their population.
4. For a document, read it in its delivery medium; links and citations resolve.
5. A goal change or a one-way choice is ESCALATE.
