# Owner Step Contract

A question beginning with `Owner step: <exact command> (<12 lowercase hex token>)` may carry trailing context. Reply lookup uses the full asked question; authorization uses the leading identity. No or free text replaces an earlier Yes. A different command, missing token, seat answer or consumed Yes cannot authorize the action.

A related mismatched answer produces the expected card and a reason identifying the shared command or token and which expected component failed. This applies to build next and dispatch as well as ordinary Owner step refusals. Unrelated cards produce no mismatch hint.
